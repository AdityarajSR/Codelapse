What Codelapse is (and an accuracy note)

Codelapse is a free educational tool that visualizes how code runs, step by step. For Python and JavaScript/TypeScript, your code executes entirely in your own browser — nothing you type is uploaded to run.

The visualization is a teaching aid that may simplify or approximate real runtime behaviour (for example, concurrency, floating-point detail, and memory layout). It is not a substitute for a real debugger, and it comes with no warranty of accuracy. Don’t rely on it for anything safety-critical.

Privacy

Codelapse has no accounts and no user database. We don’t ask for your name or email, and we don’t store the code you run on our servers (Python/JS/TS run in your browser).

  • IP addresses may be processed transiently by our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) and any anti-abuse checks purely to keep the service available and prevent abuse. They are not used to build a profile of you and are retained only briefly.
  • Analytics are cookieless and aggregate (Cloudflare Web Analytics) — no cross-site tracking, no advertising cookies.
  • Essential cookies set by Cloudflare for security / bot-mitigation may be present; these are strictly necessary to operate the service.
  • AI assistant. The “This step” and “Quiz” tabs work entirely in your browser. When you use Ask AI, your code, your question, the chat so far and a snapshot of the current step are sent to our server and passed to an AI provider (Google’s Gemini API) to generate the answer. We don’t store them; the provider processes them under its own terms and privacy policy, and on Google’s free tier this content may be used to improve Google’s products. Don’t paste secrets or personal data into code you ask about.
  • Voice. The microphone buttons use your browser’s built-in speech recognition; Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft to turn it into text. We never receive or store audio. Reading answers aloud happens on your device.
  • Shared links carry your code (and optionally a recorded run) inside the link itself — they are not stored on our servers. Anyone with the link can open it.

Under India’s DPDP Act 2023 and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have rights over personal data. Because we hold essentially none, most requests are moot — but you can reach us below.
